## Scoring & Practice Apps

### Official & Popular

- [DartConnect](https://www.dartconnect.com) - Popular scoring app for online play, leagues, tournaments, and video scoring.
- [DartCounter](https://dartcounter.co.uk) - World's leading darts platform with online play, DartBots, game modes, and full match statistics.
- [Dartsva](https://dartsva.com) - Personal darts coach with structured 6-week training plans (Road to 30-70 average), 2-tap scoring, free X01 multiplayer, and free practice tools.
- [Nakka N01](https://nakka.com) - Free, polished soft-tip darts scoring program (X01, Cricket, and more) by Tomoaki Ohno.
- [Russ Bray Darts Scorer](https://play.google.com/store/search?q=russ%20bray%20darts&c=apps) - Mobile scorer featuring the iconic voice of darts' most famous caller.

### Open Source Scorers

- [dascr-board](https://github.com/dascr/dascr-board) - Darts scorekeeping application (successor of dart-o-mat-3000) built with Svelte.

### Practice Games & Routines

- [Dartn.de](https://dartn.de) - German darts community with in-depth guides to practice games like Bob's 27, Around the Clock, and check-out training.

## Calculators & Statistics

### Checkout Finders

- [Dart Outshot](https://dartoutshot.com) - Find the best checkout for every score from 2 to 501, including PDC standard routes and smart alternatives.
- [MiniWebTool Dart Checkout Calculator](https://miniwebtool.com/dart-checkout-calculator/) - Recommended 1, 2, or 3-dart outs with an animated board highlighting exactly where to aim.
- [TheDartScout](https://thedartscout.com/darts-checkout-calculator/) - Interactive checkout calculator that recalculates the best finishing route in real time as you throw.

### Statistics & Match Analysis

- [Darts Database](https://www.dartsdatabase.co.uk) - The ultimate archive of results and player profiles spanning PDC, WDF, BDO, and regional tournaments.
- [Darts Rankings](https://www.dartsrankings.com) - Live PDC Order of Merit and race-to-? tables, updated after every tournament.
- [Dartsnews.com](https://dartsnews.com) - Daily PDC and WDF news with standings, prize money breakdowns, and results.

## DIY & Hardware

### Computer-Vision Auto-Scoring

- [DartCaller/darts-recognition](https://github.com/DartCaller/darts-recognition) - Python image processing to extract dart scores from photos.
- [DartScore](https://github.com/teddycool/DartScore) - Raspberry Pi-based automatic dart scoring system.
- [dart-sense](https://github.com/bnww/dart-sense) - Deep-learning scoring app that predicts dart positions from a smartphone video stream with automatic board calibration.
- [deep-darts](https://github.com/wmcnally/deep-darts) - Deep-learning-based automatic scoring for steel-tip darts from a single image at any camera angle.
- [opencv-steel-darts](https://github.com/hanneshoettinger/opencv-steel-darts) - Automatic scoring using OpenCV, a Raspberry Pi, and two webcams.

### Smart Dartboards & Electronic Scoring

- [Gran Board](https://granboard.com) - Electronic dartboards with camera-based auto-scoring and online multiplayer.
- [Target Omni](https://www.target-darts.co.uk) - Smart steel-tip dartboard with camera-based automatic scoring, integrated with DartCounter.

### 3D Printing & Accessories

- Print flight protectors, case inserts, and oche/board mounts on your own rig - check Printables/Thingiverse and r/Darts for community designs. Contributions to this section are welcome.

## Professional Darts & Organizations

### Governing Bodies

- [PDC - Professional Darts Corporation](https://www.pdc.tv) - The world's leading darts tour: rankings, tournaments, and news.
- [WDF - World Darts Federation](https://dartswdf.com) - Global governing body and the home of the Lakeside World Championship.

### News & Results

- [Darts Database](https://www.dartsdatabase.co.uk) - Live scores, historical results, and tournament archives.
- [Dartsnews.com](https://dartsnews.com) - Latest PDC and WDF coverage, draws, and results.
- [Darts Rankings](https://www.dartsrankings.com) - Live Order of Merit and race tables.

Major tournaments include the PDC World Darts Championship, Premier League Darts, World Matchplay, Grand Slam of Darts, UK Open, World Grand Prix, and the WDF's Lakeside World Championship.

## Gear, Equipment & Buying Guides

### Brands

- [Mission Darts](https://missiondarts.com) - Modern darts brand from the makers of Red Dragon.
- [Red Dragon](https://www.reddragondarts.com) - Popular, great-value manufacturer and retailer of darts and accessories.
- [Target](https://www.target-darts.co.uk) - Premium darts with signature player ranges, K-Flex, and Swiss Points.
- [Unicorn](https://www.unicorndarts.com) - Historic brand and long-time board supplier for world championships.
- [Winmau](https://www.winmau.com) - Manufacturer of the official PDC board, the Winmau Blade series.

### Setup & Buying Guides

- [DartCounter Manual](https://dartcounter.net) - Rules, scoring, and setup reference.
- Board setup standards: bullseye center at 5 ft 8 in (1.73 m) above the floor, and an oche distance of 7 ft 9.25 in (2.37 m) for steel-tip play.

## Developer Resources

### Libraries

- [Lanthanum89/darts-calculator](https://github.com/Lanthanum89/darts-calculator) - Checkout calculator suggesting optimal finishes for any score from 2 to 170, with CLI, GUI, and web interfaces.
- [rg3/darts](https://github.com/rg3/darts) - C++ dart game checkout solver.

### APIs & Datasets

- No official public PDC/WDF API exists yet - community-maintained results are the best source today (see [Darts Database](https://www.dartsdatabase.co.uk)). Contributions of scraping projects or datasets are welcome.
